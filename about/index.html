About kimpau

kimpau is our family blog. We’re Kim and Paula Lovino, living in Maple Ridge, BC with our three daughters — Lia, Oriana and Quea.

We share our family life as @kimpauever on YouTube and Instagram. Here on kimpau.com, we write the longer version: what we’re learning as parents, what’s working in our home, and the things we genuinely love and use.

This site earns from ads and affiliate links, clearly disclosed on every post that has them. That helps us keep writing and filming without charging for it.

Thanks for reading — and for being part of our family’s story.
