Welcome to kimpau

Hi — we’re Kim and Paula Lovino, parents to Lia, Oriana and Quea in Maple Ridge, BC.

We started sharing our family life as @kimpauever on YouTube and Instagram. This blog is the slower, more useful version of that: the things we wish we’d known, the products that actually survived three little girls, the outings we go back to, and the routines that make our days work.

What you’ll find here

  • Honest favourites — gear, books, toys and home things we actually use
  • Family outings and simple ideas for days out with little kids
  • Small rhythms: meals, mornings, bedtime, and keeping a busy house calm
  • Links to our latest videos at @kimpauever

How this site earns money

kimpau is an affiliate blog. That means two things:

1. Affiliate links. Some links here go to stores like Amazon. If you buy through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend things we use ourselves or would happily buy for our own family.

2. Ads. You’ll see ads on the site as it grows. They help keep the blog free to read.

We’ll always say so clearly on any post with affiliate links, and our full disclosure is here: Affiliate Disclosure.

Thanks for being here. This is just the start — the next posts will be the useful ones.

— Kim & Paula
